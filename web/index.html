Monk-E Bars

Lexical, hashtag and account analysis of scraped social posts.

The files are read inside this browser tab and are never uploaded. A spreadsheet needs only a column of post text; likes, comments and dates are matched by name where they exist. Captures from two platforms are refused in one analysis, because pooling them would rank two engagement scales against each other.

Settings

The capture is read once. These only filter it, so a change applies at once.
