Monk-E Bars

Lexical, hashtag and account analysis of scraped social posts.

Nothing is uploaded: the files are read inside this tab. The platform is read from the file, and a spreadsheet needs only a column of post text. An Instagram and a TikTok capture can be worked in one session: each platform is ranked inside itself, because likes on one are not likes on the other.
